# The 30-second opening (use on every video)

Strangers decide in about 5 seconds. On the Sydney video, 60% left almost immediately, but the people who stayed watched nearly 8 minutes. The middle of your videos works. This template fixes the front door.

## The template

| Time | Line | What it does | Fill-in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **0:00–0:05** | **HOOK** | Your most dramatic moment, plus ONE spoken sentence | "[the scariest / most honest thing you said or felt]" |
| **0:05–0:20** | **STAKES** | Why it matters and what could go wrong | "Hi friends. [where I was] → [what I'm trying to do] → [what could go wrong]." |
| **0:20–0:30** | **PROMISE** | What they get if they stay | "Stay with me, because by the end you'll see [the moment / the result / the lesson]." |

**Rules**
- The first words are spoken in the first 5 seconds. No title card, logo or music-only intro.
- "Hi friends" goes at the *start of the stakes line*, not before the hook.
- Say the stakes out loud. Showing them isn't enough.
- Promise something the video really delivers, and deliver it.
- After 0:30, montages are fine, but talk over them. Music-only stretches are capped at 20 seconds.

**Recording tip:** film the stakes and promise lines last, to camera, once you know how the story ended. It takes 5 minutes and you can do it at home.

## Example 1: Sydney Marathon (from real footage)

> **HOOK (0:00):** *[the 0:29 clip]* "I don't know if I can do this with my leg."
>
> **STAKES (0:05):** "Hi friends. [CHECK: how long ago / how bad the IT band injury was, e.g. 'A few months ago I couldn't run at all.'] Now I'm in Sydney, about to run my first World Marathon Major, 42.2 kilometres, on a leg I still don't fully trust."
>
> **PROMISE (0:20):** "Stay with me from race week all the way to the finish line, because [CHECK: what really happened, e.g. 'kilometre 30 nearly broke me']."

*This is the line the Sydney trim moves to the front: cut everything before 0:29. See doc 06.*

## Example 2: IT band comeback

> **HOOK (0:00):** *[CHECK: the most honest moment, e.g. a clip of you stopping mid-run, or your first pain-free stride]* "[CHECK: what you really said, e.g. 'I honestly thought I might not run again.']"
>
> **STAKES (0:05):** "Hi friends. An IT band injury took running away from me for [CHECK: how long]. This isn't the highlight reel. This is my real return to running, including the days it didn't go well."
>
> **PROMISE (0:20):** "By the end you'll see my first [CHECK: distance] back, and what actually helped me get there."

## Example 3: Faith in Motion (Ep. 2, Matthew 6:33)

> **HOOK (0:00):** *[you mid-run, breathing hard, looking at the camera]* "If I could give every Christian one life hack, it would be this verse."
>
> **STAKES (0:05):** "Hi friends. Matthew 6:33: 'But seek first his kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well.' [CHECK: your translation.] Honestly, I've been putting [CHECK: what you were seeking first: training, work, the next goal] ahead of God, and I felt it."
>
> **PROMISE (0:20):** "So run with me today, and I'll show you what putting this verse first actually looks like in a normal, busy week."

## Bonus: follow-along opening (no drama needed)

> **HOOK (0:00):** *[you, ready to go, looking straight at the camera]* "Hi friends, grab your shoes. For the next 30 minutes you're running with me, and I'll pray with you at the end."
>
> **STAKES (0:05):** "If you can't get yourself out the door today, this one is for you. Easy pace. You should be able to talk."
>
> **PROMISE (0:20):** "By the end you'll have 30 minutes done and one verse to carry into your week. Today it's [verse]."
